Project Concept & Experience Disclaimer

This website presents a project concept and possible feature ideas for discussion with Mac PC Rescue. The layouts, workflows, example data, statuses, pricing fields, and preview interfaces are demonstrations only and may change before or during development.

This concept is not presented as professional consulting or professional service-industry system design. Bow and Potter Service do not represent themselves as professionals in the repair-service management field. The ideas shown are proposed from a practical project-development perspective for client review, feedback, and approval of the general direction.

Concept approval means approval of the general direction.

It does not mean every feature shown must be built, that every demonstration screen becomes the final design, or that all proposed features belong in the first release. Final scope, technical feasibility, security, integrations, phases, and requirements should be confirmed before development.
