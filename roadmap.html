PROJECT ROADMAP

From foundation to full service system.

This roadmap combines the original development plan with the completed MPR Service System phase history. It documents the system’s growth from the Phase 3 database foundation through Phase 14.0 returns and refunds.

DEVELOPMENT PLAN

Proposed development stages.

01 • Project & Website

Organize the project, public website, concept information, hosting approach, branding, and requirements.

02 • Database Foundation

Plan SQL structure, authentication, settings, service catalog, customers, devices, and ticket foundations.

03 • Admin Portal

Develop the staff side for tickets, queue, diagnostics, billing, customers, devices, settings, and permissions.

04 • Client Portal

Add secure customer-facing status, appropriate updates, invoice information, and payment capabilities.

05 • Website Connection

Connect public service requests to the backend so staff can review and convert inquiries into customers and tickets.

06 • Optional Kiosk

Add the in-store customer experience if Mac PC Rescue chooses to include it.

07 • Integrations

Add approved payment, printing, and other integrations after core workflows are stable.

08 • Future Expansion

Possible notifications, mobile/PWA tools, status-only experience, and other approved additions.
